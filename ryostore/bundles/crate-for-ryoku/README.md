# Crate for Ryoku

Crate's look, and Crate itself, on Ryoku: the Crate rice (palette, type,
wallpapers, fastfetch emblem), the crate-mark bar plugin with now playing and
Crate's equalizer, every app icon redrawn as a Solar glyph, and the terminal
(kitty, eza, the prompt) in Crate's palette.

This bundle installs:

- `crate-desktop-bin`: the Crate desktop app (AUR).
- `crate-for-ryoku`: the package that holds the pieces (AUR).
- the `crate-for-ryoku` setup, which lays them into your home and applies the
  rice (`crate-for-ryoku install`; undo with `crate-for-ryoku uninstall`).

Both packages come from the AUR, so the bundle is offered on Arch and CachyOS.
Source, issues and the full description:
https://github.com/thecrateapp/crate-for-ryoku (MIT; Solar icons CC BY 4.0).
